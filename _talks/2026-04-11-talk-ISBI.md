During the International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI), I gave a 8-minute oral presentation to introduce the contributions of our paper intitled _NC-Reg : Neural Cortical Maps for Rigid Registration_. In this work, we proposed a novel neural implicit representation of spherical cortical features maps and show its promising application by using it in a novel algorithm for rotational alignment. 

This conference is a leading international forum in the field of biomedical imaging, bringing together researchers and professionals from academia and industry. In addition to the paper presentation, I attended a tutorial and some workshops organized as part of the conference program. These sessions focused on emerging methodologies, tools, and applications in biomedical imaging and provided valuable technical training and insight into the latest developments in the field. I summarized my main takeaways in this [blog post](https://inesvati.github.io/posts/2026/04/25/takeaway-ISBI).
