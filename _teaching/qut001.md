I am a member of the teaching team in [QUT001](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=QUT001) unit. It is part of the QUT You program that allows students from different year levels and faculties including Engineering, Business, Health, Law and Design to work together on broad real-world issues.
This unit explored what Artificial Intelligent is, how it is developed, and its potential pitfalls. 
With another tutor in the classroom, we taught students to decipher AI jargon, probe AI systems to understand their capabilites and limitations. The purpose of this class was to raise awareness about how AI may impact their future career, and the challenges to ensure that AI systems acts fairly and ethically. 
I provided help and support during tutorials and assignment sessions.
