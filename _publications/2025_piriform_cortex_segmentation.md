During my 6-month internship at CSIRO in 2025, I had the opportunity to briefly help the talented researchers that worked on this project. The work focuses on automated piriform cortex (PC) segmentation on MRI scans. They compare state-of-the-art deep learning (DL) models such as Transformer-inspired models (MedNeXtL, SwinUNETR, etc.)
